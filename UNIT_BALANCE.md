# Adding and balancing units

The standard roster uses `defineUnit` in `src/game/config.ts`. Choose a food cost, role profile, movement speed, range and attack interval. Health, damage per hit and coin price are generated by `src/game/balance.ts`.

## Shared power budget

Let `F` be food cost, `H` the role's health bias, and `U` its range/movement multiplier:

```text
budget = F ^ 1.2
U = (1 + max(0, range - 25) / 1500) × (speed / 39) ^ 0.2
health = round(100 × budget × H / U)
DPS = (15 / 0.9) × budget / (H × U)
damage per hit = round(DPS × attack interval)
coin price = nearest 25 to [100 × (F - 1) ^ 1.2]
```

The starter costs zero coins. Unit food costs are consecutive whole numbers from 1 to 21, with Stormblade at 4, Mech at 5, Dark Mage at 20, and Jetpack Raccoon at 21 after closing the retired-unit gap. Stored battle food has no cap. Food cost is the primary in-battle budget; coin price is the one-time progression cost. Owned units remain owned when prices change.

Health and DPS trade against each other rather than both rising in a straight line. Before rounding, their geometric mean has the same adjusted value for units with the same food cost. Range and faster movement consume a modest share of that budget. Range allowance is 1500 rather than the previous 250; long reach must not erase the raw health/DPS gain of an extra food tier. Attack interval converts DPS into individual hits: a rapid Machine Gunner deals smaller hits more frequently, while a slow heavy weapon deals larger hits. The exponent 1.2 gives larger units increasing combined HP/DPS value per food, making gold unlocks worthwhile. Doubling food supplies about 2.30x health and DPS within the same role and utility profile. Tank roles can still have lower DPS per food because they buy substantially more health. Enemy creatures use defineEnemyUnit with their original exponent 0.8, so player purchases do not also strengthen waves.

| Profile | Health bias H | Job |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| balanced | 1.00 | General-purpose fighter |
| tank | 1.80 | More health, lower DPS |
| damage | 0.75 | Less health, higher DPS |
| glassCannon | 0.55 | Very fragile, highest DPS |

Speed must be 20–60, range 25–260, and attack interval 0.12–2 seconds. The lower attack-interval bound supports rapid weapons; per-hit damage still derives from the same DPS budget. Unsupported values fail early instead of creating an unaffordable or invalid unit. These bounds are tuning limits, not a substitute for combat checks.

## Roster progression guard

Raw combat power is the geometric mean of normalized health and sustained DPS:

`sqrt((HP / 100) × (DPS / (15 / 0.9)))`.

Every current roster tier must increase this value by at least `(new food / previous food) ^ 0.3`, before credit for range or speed. It must also improve either health or DPS, so a higher-priced card cannot be lower in both. Role bias still trades health against DPS; neither stat is required to rise on every tier. Every advanced purchase must also exceed the best starter unit in combined power per food by at least 5%. One Machine Gunner must have more DPS and health than two Rock Throwers and win that six-food duel in both attack orders.

Roster and army stats show rounded damage per hit, including damage upgrades, without /s. For Mech this is the combined damage of its two-missile volley. DPS remains part of the balance formula and report so attack speed is accounted for.

## Add a unit

For example, a future five-food tank could be defined as:

```ts
guardian: defineUnit({
  name: "Guardian",
  role: "Tank",
  foodCost: 5,
  profile: "tank",
  speed: 30,
  range: 25,
  cooldown: 0.8,
  projectile: null,
}),
```

Do not hand-enter health, damage, or unlock price. Add its simple weapon art in `src/rendering/draw.ts` and card color in `src/ui/UpgradeScreen.tsx`. Player units are separate from the wave enemy pool; do not add them to `AI.weights`. New unit keys stay locked in existing saves automatically. New projectile types also need a projectile type and renderer. Check their framing in shop/deployment portraits.

Also add its body-front extent and, for melee, its animated weapon-tip reach to `src/game/geometry.ts`, in unscaled model coordinates. The shared battlefield scale (1.4) and boss scale convert those extents to combat distances. Melee contact is weapon reach plus target body front minus three model pixels of allowed contact; blueprint `range` remains a stat-budget input rather than a center-to-center melee hit distance. Movement also stops at opposing body bounds. Ranged attack distances use their configured range and the player bonus below, with a body-contact floor for large targets.

Player ranged troops receive a 1.2x attack-distance multiplier in `geometry.ts`, measured to the target’s actual scaled body edge, including normal enemies and bosses. Ranged support pulls back behind a living melee screen when the enemy closes, with a 23-pixel dead zone and bunker/rear-ally limits. Lone ranged units remain vulnerable to being caught. Blueprint ranges still set the existing HP/DPS budget, so the reach bonus does not reduce health or damage or change prices. Enemy ranged units and boss attack distances keep their existing values.

## Verify the result

Run `npm run typecheck`, `npm test`, and `npm run balance`. The last command writes `artifacts/unit-balance-report.md`, including the generated stats, DPS per food, combined power per food, normalized budget ratio and equal-food matchup matrix.

Tests cover all 20 food budgets across four profiles and three ranges, ranged/movement tradeoffs, cooldown changes, strictly increasing roster power, progression prices and invalid inputs. The current twenty-unit roster also runs 380 equal-food fights with attack priority reversed. Every advanced purchase must win at least one starter matchup. Starter units are no longer required to beat later unlocks at equal food; that old requirement worked against progression. Enemy base stats are separately guarded against accidental roster buffs. These are reproducible guardrails; deployment timing, mixed armies, overkill and first-hit thresholds still need live playtesting when adding a unit.











## Final Jetpack Raccoon

The final 10,000-gold unlock is an explicit exception to the shared food-budget curve, as requested: 5× Dark Mage health and 5× sustained DPS. It derives both from Dark Mage (10,250 HP, 1,581 damage every 0.28 seconds), rather than duplicating hand-entered stats. It costs 21 food, moves at 30, and has 240 base range (288 player reach). The normal roster and enemy budgets are unchanged. Tests separately enforce its exact five-times power and keep the standard roster normalization guard. Rifle impacts are small cosmetic explosions without splash damage.
